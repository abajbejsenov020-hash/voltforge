# Stock vehicle profiles

Updated 2026-11-18. VOLTMODD now has a stock reference for **all 355 catalog entries**. **331 electric vehicles** show speed, peak power, estimated range, and estimated ride time before any parts are selected. **21** show the figures their sources support, with the remaining fields flagged in the build. **3** are pedal-powered bikes.

This pass audited the remaining 195 entries and brought 171 more electric vehicles to all four figures. None of the 160 previously complete builds lost coverage. Among the 331 complete builds, 319 have a published speed or pedal-assist cutoff, and 255 have a published peak output. The remaining speed and power figures are labeled planning estimates.

## How to read the figures

- [Stock profiles](../packages/shared/src/vehicles/stock-profiles.json) record the model, documented trim and market, the technical source, and its conditions. Supporting sources appear in `sources`. Null or absent values represent unverified specifications.
- Speed is expressed in km/h. Assisted bicycles show their factory assist cutoff. A published reference reflects the named trim, regional configuration, and firmware settings recorded in its profile. For example, the Cube Touring Hybrid profile uses Bosch’s 600 W factory setting for the Performance Line PX. [Bosch factory and upgrade settings](https://www.bosch-ebike.com/en/performance-upgrade).
- `ratedMotorW` is the published continuous or nominal power per motor. `peakMotorW` is the published maximum for the full system. A peak whose test duration or measurement basis is unpublished is labeled as a reference. Where only continuous power is established, the calculator labels the assumed burst output as a rough estimate. An electrical ceiling remains a ceiling.
- Range and ride time are scenario estimates at the selected cruising speed, using usable pack energy, vehicle and rider mass, and modeled consumption. The estimate for a pedal-assist bike excludes rider power. Manufacturer range figures remain in the source profile with their test conditions. Ride time comes from estimated range divided by cruise speed.
- Wh may come from published pack energy or from published nominal voltage × capacity in Ah. When only Wh and nominal volts are published, a transient capacity equivalent can drive the model. The catalog leaves source Ah blank. Loaded voltage and battery-side current are independent measurements.
- Tire sizes are nominal outside diameters where published. Motorcycle tire codes can support a calculated unloaded outside diameter. Rim diameters and unresolved sizes stay blank.

G4 retains its factory 60 V, 20 Ah, single 2 kW rated motor, 2.4 kW peak, and 70 km/h reference. [KuKirin G4 specifications](https://www.kukirinfactory.com/kukirin-g4-specification).

# Arctic Leopard E-ZPRO

Junior electric enduro designed for off-road riding – a compact electric dirt bike with an output of up to 2.5 kW, three speed modes, and ergonomics designed for young riders.

## A real electric dirt bike for young riders

The Arctic Leopard E-ZPRO brings the technology and experience of the Arctic Leopard brand to the junior electric off-road motorcycle category. It is designed as a natural transition between a lightweight electric balance bike or children's electric motorcycle and a real off-road motorcycle.

The E-ZPRO is not just a children's balance bike equipped with a stronger motor. It offers the construction of a real dirt bike, hydraulic disc brakes on both wheels, off-road ready suspension, chain drive, and performance that allows for the gradual development of riding skills.

Compact dimensions, a seat height of 650 mm, and a wheelbase of 1050 mm correspond to the proportions of younger riders. As a result, the motorcycle is easier to control at lower speeds, during direction changes, and when practicing the correct riding position.

The electric motor offers a nominal output of 1.5 kW and a peak output of up to 2.5 kW. The torque on the rear wheel reaches up to 220 Nm, giving the E-ZPRO plenty of power for off-road riding, hill climbs, and smooth acceleration out of corners.

Power is transmitted via a chain without the need to use a clutch or shift gears. This allows the rider to focus primarily on balance, throttle control, the correct line, and safe braking.

A major advantage of the E-ZPRO model is its three selectable speed modes limiting the maximum speed to 25, 35, or 45 km/h. The settings can be adapted to the rider's experience, the character of the track, and the current stage of learning.

The 48 V battery with a capacity of 20 Ah offers a total energy of 960 Wh. The reference range can reach up to 65 km, but the actual value always depends on speed, rider weight, terrain profile, temperature, and acceleration frequency.

The electric drive operates quietly, without burning fuel, and without a hot exhaust system. Compared to a combustion-engine children's motorcycle, this eliminates shifting, clutch work, refueling, and the risk of contact with a hot exhaust.

The Arctic Leopard E-ZPRO is intended exclusively for off-road riding. The offered off-road version is not homologated for road use and is not intended for registration or riding on public roads, sidewalks, bike paths, or other publicly accessible routes.

## Three speed modes for gradual development

The Arctic Leopard E-ZPRO allows the maximum speed to be limited in three stages. This enables the motorcycle's settings to be gradually adapted to the rider's abilities without needing to purchase a more powerful machine with every improvement.

The mode with a maximum speed of 25 km/h is suitable for initial familiarization with the motorcycle, practicing throttle control, and riding in a clear training area. However, it is still a powerful off-road motorcycle that requires appropriate rider skills and adult supervision.

The middle mode, with speeds up to 35 km/h, allows for further development of riding technique, smoother cornering, and practicing riding in more rugged terrain.

The highest mode unlocks a maximum speed of up to 45 km/h and is intended only for more experienced riders who already safely master acceleration, braking, direction changes, and the correct riding position.

Choosing a lower speed limit does not in itself replace safe training, a suitable environment, protective equipment, or the supervision of a responsible adult.


## 48 V / 20 Ah battery and a range of up to 65 km

The E-ZPRO model is equipped with a battery featuring a voltage of 48 V and a capacity of 20 Ah, which corresponds to a total energy of 960 Wh. This capacity provides an adequate reserve for regular practice riding and active use off public roads.

The reference maximum range reaches up to 65 km. In real off-road operation, however, the range is often significantly affected by soft or broken surfaces, uphill riding, frequent acceleration, low outdoor temperatures, and rider weight.

During sports off-road riding, it is therefore advisable to assess battery life not only by kilometers but also by the intensity and length of a specific training session. A calm ride on a firm surface consumes significantly less energy than repeated starts, climbs, and riding in deep or soft terrain.

To maintain good battery condition, it is necessary to use only the designated charger, avoid exposing the battery to extreme temperatures, and not leave it stored in a completely discharged state for long periods.


The Arctic Leopard E-ZPRO is not a toy, but a real electric off-road motorcycle. A maximum speed of up to 45 km/h, immediate power delivery, and a weight of 46 kg require corresponding experience, physical proportions, and the rider's ability to safely control the motorcycle.

Suitability for a specific rider cannot be determined by age alone. Crucial factors include height, the ability to safely reach the controls and the ground, physical strength, the ability to brake, and previous experience riding a bicycle, electric balance bike, or motorcycle.

The rider must always wear appropriate protective equipment, especially a high-quality full-face or motocross helmet, goggles, gloves, sturdy footwear, knee and elbow pads, and a suitable torso or spine protector.

The riding of a young rider must take place under the supervision of a responsible adult. The first rides must be conducted in the lowest speed mode, in a flat and clear area without obstacles, traffic, or other people.

## Off-road version for tracks and closed areas

The Arctic Leopard E-ZPRO is not homologated for use on public roads. It is not a road vehicle, and the motorcycle is not intended for registration for regular road traffic.

The motorcycle is intended exclusively for use off public roads, especially on closed off-road and children's MX tracks, in training areas, private grounds, and on properties where the operation of motor vehicles is explicitly permitted.

Private land ownership alone does not automatically mean the motorcycle can be operated on it. The consent of the owner or operator is always required, along with compliance with valid legal regulations, local restrictions, and the operating rules of the specific area.

The motorcycle must not be used on public roads, local roads, sidewalks, bike paths, or any other publicly accessible routes. This restriction applies in all speed modes, including the mode with a maximum speed of 25 km/h.

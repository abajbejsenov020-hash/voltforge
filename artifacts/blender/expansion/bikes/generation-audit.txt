# The XP Lite Evolution: More Features, Same $799 Starting Price

October 27, 2026

Three generations. Four years of innovation. One seriously good value.

When we introduced the original XP Lite in 2022, the goal was simple: make eBike ownership more accessible with a ride that was lightweight, easy to store, and easy on the wallet.

Since then, we've continued to build on that formula. The XP Lite2 brought meaningful upgrades to power delivery, braking, and everyday comfort. Now, the all-new XP Lite3 takes things even further with premium features like hydraulic brakes, an advanced color TFT display, integrated turn signals, and an optional Gates Carbon Belt Drive.

The best part? The XP Lite still starts at just $799.

That's right. Even as we've added more technology, improved the riding experience, and introduced features typically associated with more expensive eBikes, we've kept the entry price exactly where it started.

Let's take a look at how the XP Lite has evolved and what makes the XP Lite3 our most feature-packed version yet.

## From the XP Lite1 to the XP Lite3: A Lot Has Changed

The original XP Lite earned its place as an approachable, portable eBike by keeping things simple. It delivered a lightweight folding design, five levels of speed-based pedal assist, and a 300W hub motor at a price that made getting started with eBikes easier.

The XP Lite2 built on that foundation with Lectric PWR™ pedal assist, a more powerful peak output, hydraulic disc brakes, a color display, and the option to choose a Gates Carbon Belt Drive instead of a traditional chain.

With the XP Lite3, we're bringing even more of those premium features together in one compact package. Riders get an upgraded color TFT display, refined assist modes, integrated turn signals, enhanced lighting, improved cockpit controls, and upgraded quick-release pedals.

And through every generation, the mission has stayed the same: deliver more ride for your money.

## 1. A Smarter, More Refined Ride with Lectric PWR™

The XP Lite's power delivery has come a long way since the original model.

The XP Lite1 used five levels of speed-based pedal assist. With the XP Lite , we introduced Lectric PWR™, a power-based pedal-assist system designed to deliver a more natural riding experience.

The XP Lite3 takes that technology further with five distinct riding modes: Eco, Tour, Sport, Sport+, and Turbo.

Each mode gives riders another way to tailor their ride to the conditions and the experience they want. Looking to stretch your battery farther? Eco gives you a more efficiency-focused option. Want a more responsive ride? Sport+ and Turbo let you access higher levels of assistance.

The XP Lite3 also retains its quiet 300W hub motor with 819W peak output, giving riders capable assistance in a compact package.

More control over your ride. More ways to make it your own. All at the same starting price.

But they're not the only option anymore.

Starting with the XP Lite2, riders could choose a Gates Carbon Belt Drive in place of the traditional chain. That option continues with the XP Lite3, giving riders another way to customize their eBike to fit their preferences with the JW Black and Ballerina Pink models.

The belt drive uses a carbon fiber-reinforced belt rather than a conventional metal chain. That means no chain grease, less routine drivetrain maintenance, and a clean, quiet riding experience.

It's especially appealing for riders who want to keep their eBike maintenance simple or prefer not to deal with greasy chains on their clothes.

The XP Lite3 keeps that flexibility intact, with chain and belt-drive configurations available.

A cleaner drivetrain. A quieter ride. Less time spent on chain maintenance.

## 4. A Color TFT Display That Puts More Information at Your Fingertips

The XP Lite's display has evolved alongside its riding technology.

The original XP Lite featured a monochrome LCD. The XP Lite2 introduced a color LCD with a USB-A port. Now, the XP Lite3 steps things up with a high-resolution color TFT display featuring advanced rider modes and a USB-C port.

Beyond a more modern look, the display offers additional ride information, including cadence, trip distance, motor wattage, amps, and voltage.

The detachable design adds another thoughtful touch, while the upgraded interface gives riders a clearer view of the information that matters to them.

It's a more advanced cockpit that makes it easier to keep tabs on your ride without overcomplicating the experience.
